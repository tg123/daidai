Privacy Policy — DaiDai Worm

Last updated: 2026-05-30

DaiDai Worm (“the Game”) is a single-player offline arcade game. This policy describes the data the Game does (and does not) collect.

What we collect

Nothing. The Game collects no personal information, no usage data, no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reports, no advertising identifiers, and no device identifiers.

What we transmit

Nothing after the initial download. The Game runs entirely on your device. There are no servers, no accounts, no online features, no in-app purchases, no third-party SDKs, and no advertising networks.

What we store

All game state (high scores, settings, language preference) is stored locally on your device only, in the application’s own sandboxed storage area — browser localStorage for the web version, the app’s private packaged-app data folder under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\ for the Microsoft Store version.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the Game (or clearing site data, for the web version) removes all stored data permanently.

Children’s privacy

The Game does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. It is safe for all ages.

Third parties

The Game uses no third-party services, SDKs, analytics, ad networks, or trackers of any kind.

Open source

The Game is fully open source under the MIT license. You can audit exactly what it does (and doesn’t do) at https://github.com/tg123/daidai.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please open an issue at https://github.com/tg123/daidai/issues or email the developer at the address listed on the Microsoft Store product page.
